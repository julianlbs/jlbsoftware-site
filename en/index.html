What JLBS Creator Dashboard does

Top-selling products

See which products generate the most sales and commission, by creator and across the whole agency.

Best time to go LIVE

A heat map of the hours when sales happen (Brasília time).

LIVE, video or showcase

Know where each sale comes from.

Commissions and payouts

Pending, settled and ineligible, with a forecast of when each amount should land.

Sales trend

Daily and weekly evolution, with comparison between periods.

Link for the creator

Each creator sees only their own earnings, through a private read-only link.

How it works

  1. The agency creates the account and connects TikTok Shop through TikTok’s official authorization.
  2. We import up to 92 days of affiliate orders and refresh the data every hour.
  3. Managers and creators follow everything in the dashboard, on phone or computer.

We use only the official TikTok Shop API, with the agency’s authorization. We do not scrape data.

Who it’s for

Creator agencies and TikTok Shop CAP partners in Brazil that manage several creators and need visibility into sales and commissions without spreadsheets.

Security and privacy

  • Data hosted in Brazil.
  • Each agency’s data isolated and encrypted.
  • Read-only access: we never change anything in your TikTok Shop account.
  • LGPD-compliant; you can request deletion of your data at any time.
